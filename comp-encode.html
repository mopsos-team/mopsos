1. Choose a text

Compound Encoder

Every word in the text has already been checked against the compound catalogue and the corpus, so most of the encoding is filled in before you start.

  • New words are highlighted with a suggested analysis. Check it and press Enter to save, or pick another suggestion.
  • Catalogue words are underlined in green. They are already analyzed and only need their attestation logged, one at a time or all at once.
  • Check words are underlined in red. The spelling fits more than one word, so they are never logged in bulk.
  • Translation: click Show translation under a passage to read Evelyn-White's English, and Hide to close it. It comes in passages of about five lines, because the translation is prose.
  • Any other word can be clicked and encoded by hand. For a text not listed, paste it in, or type a headword in Greek or Beta Code.

Fields tinted blue were filled in automatically. Change anything that is wrong; a saved compound is recognized wherever it occurs again.

Every decision is saved to the team sheet as you make it. If the connection drops, keep working: your changes are held in this browser and send on their own.

Paste Greek text, one verse per line. A number at the start of a line is read as its line number.
