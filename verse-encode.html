1. Choose a text

Verse Encoder

Every line has already been divided into syllables and scanned, and everything that can be worked out from the scansion is filled in. Your part is to check what the page was unsure of and to answer what only a reader can.

  • Scansion: click a line to open it. Click a syllable to change its weight, the length of its vowel, or where it divides. You can also pick another foot pattern or correct the text itself. The line is scanned again at once, and everything below follows.
  • Digamma: words underlined in green once began with digamma. The page says what stands before each one and whether the digamma is observed or neglected there.
  • Infinitives: words tinted yellow are active infinitives. The page puts each rival ending into the line and scans it again, so you can see which would also fit.
  • Overlong syllables, Wernicke's Law, Hermann's bridge: each case is listed under the line with the page's reading. Confirm it or correct it.

Lines marked review are the ones the page could not settle: no scansion, a costly licence, two readings that are nearly as good, or a disagreement with the scansion stored on the site. Start with those.

Every change is saved to the team sheet as you make it. If the connection drops, keep working: your changes are held in this browser and send on their own.
